## Active Projects

**[Under The Hood](https://underthehood.up.railway.app/)**  
Under The Hood tracks open-source tools by reading dependency files across 9,000+ real GitHub repositories. Instead of surveys or social chatter, it measures what teams actually install in production code, then surfaces signals on momentum, consolidation, top maintainers, and early enterprise adoption.

**[Show Me The DPI](https://show-me-the-dpi.up.railway.app/)**  
Show Me The DPI tracks realized VC/PE cash returns to LPs using public pension and endowment disclosures, supplemented by market data. The core metric is DPI (Distributed-to-Paid-In), with TVPI and IRR shown for context.

**[AI Explained For Plebs](https://aiexplainedforplebs.vercel.app/)**  
Plain-English explainers for AI concepts, built for people who want clarity without technical jargon.

**[Garam VC](https://garam-vc.vercel.app/#/leaderboard)**  
An interactive ranking board for India's VC ecosystem with a lightweight, opinionated scoring layer.

**[Dhan Ki Baat](https://www.dhankibaat.xyz/)**  
A personal investment planning tool for portfolio what-if scenarios and long-term decision support.
